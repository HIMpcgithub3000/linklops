=== what the numbers say ===
  Concurrency is the trigger: hold it constant and vary the link (candidate 1)
  and the failure follows every link; remove it and run the same clicks
  sequentially across two buckets (candidate 2) and 12/12 count correctly.

  The naive pattern has TWO failure modes, and the module only names one.
    loud   11 of 12 threads raise UniqueViolation -- all read NULL before
           any committed, so all 12 chose INSERT and 11 lost the key.
    silent 0 errors, 3 of 12 counted. Some threads read AFTER a commit,
           took the UPDATE path, and wrote count = stale_value + 1 --
           each overwriting the others. Nine clicks vanished with no
           exception, no log line, and a plausible-looking number left
           in the table.
  The silent mode is the dangerous one. A duplicate-key 500 is at least a
  page; an undercount is a dashboard nobody questions.
