=== BUG #3 CAUSE ===
  app/main.py had:  LOGGING_LEVEL_OVERRIDE = "warning"
                    configure(LOGGING_LEVEL_OVERRIDE or settings.LOG_LEVEL)

  correct, the config contract validated it, and it was discarded in code.

=== BUG #4: DATE_TRUNC('hour', NOW()) truncates in the SESSION time zone ===
  Same instant, evaluated under three session zones:
    UTC                17:00:00+00:00   |  utc-anchored  17:00:00+00:00
    Asia/Kolkata       23:00:00+05:30   |  utc-anchored  22:30:00+05:30
    America/New_York   13:00:00-04:00   |  utc-anchored  13:00:00-04:00

  BEFORE -- 3 clicks in one real hour, 2 workers in UTC and 1 in Asia/Kolkata:
    bucket 2026-08-13 17:00:00+00:00  count=2
    bucket 2026-08-13 17:30:00+00:00  count=1
    -> 2 rows for one hour. The PK is (link_id, timestamp_bucket), so
       ON CONFLICT never fired and the counts split instead of merging.

  AFTER -- DATE_TRUNC('hour', NOW() AT TIME ZONE 'UTC') AT TIME ZONE 'UTC',
  4 clicks across UTC, Asia/Kolkata and America/New_York:
    bucket 2026-08-13 17:00:00+00:00  count=4   -> 1 bucket

  Why it hides: America/New_York is a whole-hour offset, so truncating there
  and in UTC name the same instant and the bug does not exist. It only
  appears for half- and quarter-hour zones (India, Iran, Nepal, parts of
  Australia), so a team testing in US or EU zones cannot reproduce it.
